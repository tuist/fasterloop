What does building software look like when code is cheap?

Agents now write much of the code, and teams produce more of it than ever. The hard parts have moved: deciding what to build, trusting what gets merged, keeping builds and CI fast, and organizing teams around it. Nobody has this figured out yet. Faster Loop is an evening to compare notes with the people trying.

Who it's for

Engineers, platform and infrastructure teams, engineering leads, and anyone curious about where software development is going. You don't need answers, just an interest in the questions.

Questions we're exploring

  • If writing code is cheap, what becomes the hard part?
  • How do we review and trust far more changes than before?
  • What do builds, tests and CI need to look like at that volume?
  • How do codebases, teams and roles change?

Talks share what people actually tried, including what didn't work. No product pitches.

Give a talk
